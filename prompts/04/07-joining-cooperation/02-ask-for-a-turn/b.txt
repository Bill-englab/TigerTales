START FRAME

ACTION AND REACTIONS
Dad rolls the ball once from his left hand into his waiting right palm — exactly the single agreed final roll — and stops it fully. Only then does he lift the ball a few centimeters and place it directly into Child's open hands; this transfer is not another solo roll. Child receives it before thanking him and announcing his plan to roll it back.
